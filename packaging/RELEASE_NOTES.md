## What's new

- The player runs 2.55 and 3.0 Nightly quests
- Fixed the magenta strip under some item screens
- Quests can be kept in folders with `#` in the name
- ZQuest refuses quests newer than 2.53.2 until the 2.55/3.0 editor is finished

## Known gaps

- Slopes and z-heights are not supported yet
- Lifting, the switch hook, new dark rooms and some new combo types have not been confirmed in play
- The Android app starts, but 2.55 and 3.0 Nightly quests have not been tested on Android yet

Unzip the Windows download somewhere you can write to, then run `zplayer.exe` or `zqeditor.exe`.
