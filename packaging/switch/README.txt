ZC Vulkan 2.53.444.6-PRE
Nintendo Switch homebrew

The Zelda Classic player for Switch homebrew. It plays 2.53 quests and quests
made in 2.55 and the 3.0 Nightly builds. ZQuest, the quest editor, isn't part
of this build.

Installing
  Copy the "switch" folder from this zip to the root of your SD card, so the
  game ends up in sdmc:/switch/zplayer/. Start zplayer from the Homebrew Menu.
  Put your quests, and any music they come with, in sdmc:/switch/zplayer/.

Controls
  D-pad or left stick   Move
  A, B                  A, B
  Plus                  Start
  Minus                 System menu
  L, R                  L, R
  ZL, ZR, X, Y          Extra buttons 1 to 4
  Left stick click      Map

Player settings are stored in zc.cfg and saved games in zc.sav.

Tested so far in the Eden emulator only, not on a real Switch.
